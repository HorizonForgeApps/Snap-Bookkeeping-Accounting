What's inside

Bookkeeping, done properly

Most receipt scanners give you a list of amounts sorted by category. Snap Bookkeeping gives you a real chart of accounts, a general ledger, and a trial balance that actually has to balance — the same structure your accountant already works in.

Everything below is available from day one of your trial. Nothing gets paywalled later.

§ 01

Real double-entry ledgers

Every posted transaction carries a matching debit and credit into a proper chart of accounts — Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, and Expenses — not a flat list dressed up to look like one.

§ 02

AI reads the receipt for you

Point the camera and the app extracts vendor, date, each line item, and VAT, then suggests a category — you just confirm it before anything posts.

§ 03

VAT split out automatically

Input and output VAT post to their own control accounts, at your country's rate and label — VAT, GST, or Sales Tax — so it's never buried inside an expense figure.

§ 04

Bank statement reconciliation

Upload a statement and match each line against an invoice you've already captured, instead of counting the same amount twice.

§ 05

One-tap trial balance

Pick a date range and get a proper trial balance in two sections — balance sheet and income statement — each with its own live check that debits equal credits. Export as CSV or PDF, or print it.

§ 06

Works with no signal

Capture and manual entry never depend on a connection. If AI extraction can't reach the network, it queues quietly and retries once you're back online.

§ 07

Your own AI quota, your own data

Extraction runs on your own free Google AI Studio key, so it's never shared or rate-limited by other users, and there's nothing for us to bill you for.

§ 08

Easy to fix, no audit-trail maze

Made a mistake? Edit or delete the entry directly. This is built for a small business owner correcting their own books, not a formal audit workflow.

Questions worth answering upfront

Does this file my tax return for me?+

No. Snap Bookkeeping prepares an accurate, well-organized set of books — a chart of accounts, ledgers, and a trial balance. Filing the actual return is still a job for you or your accountant; think of this as doing all the work that comes before that step.

Where does my receipt photo actually go?+

It's stored on your device. When you use AI-assisted capture, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API using an API key you provide yourself — it's never routed through our servers, and we don't see it. Full detail is in our privacy policy.

What's this "own API key" thing about?+

Google gives every account a free daily quota for the AI model that reads your receipts. Instead of sharing one quota across every Snap Bookkeeping user — which would mean rate limits, or a subscription price built around our AI bill — you connect your own free key from Google AI Studio in Settings. It takes about a minute and costs nothing.

What happens if I don't have signal?+

Capture and manual entry work fully offline. If AI extraction can't reach the network, it queues the document and finishes the read automatically once you're back online — nothing is lost.

Does it handle VAT, GST, or sales tax?+

Yes. Pick your country and currency in Settings, and the app applies the right label and rate — VAT, GST, or Sales Tax — and posts it to its own control accounts, separate from the expense or income figure.

Can I try it before paying?+

Every feature is unlocked for 30 days, free, no card gymnastics or feature gates. There's also a pre-loaded demo business you can explore before you touch your own numbers.

Is there an ad-supported free version?+

No — and that's deliberate. Your financial data isn't used to sell you anything, in this app or anywhere else. One subscription, everything included.
