1. Overview

Snap Bookkeeping & Accounting ("the app," "we," "us") is a bookkeeping tool built by Horizon Forge for small business owners, freelancers, and contractors. This policy explains what data the app touches, where it goes, and what control you have over it.

The short version: your books live on your device. The only third party your data is ever sent to is Google, for the specific job of reading a receipt photo — and that happens through your own Google account, not ours. We don't run ads, we don't sell data, and we don't have a server that stores your ledger.

2. What data the app handles

The app is built to work without any account, sign-up, or login. It does not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information to use the core bookkeeping features. Within the app, you may choose to enter:

  • Business information you type in yourself — business name, address, contact details, currency, and VAT/GST settings — used to label your own reports and printed statements.
  • Photos and files of receipts, invoices, credit notes, and bank statements that you capture or upload.
  • Ledger data — the vendors, customers, amounts, categories, and journal entries created as you use the app.
  • A Google AI Studio (Gemini) API key, if you choose to enable AI-assisted extraction — this is your own key, tied to your own Google account, entered voluntarily in Settings.

None of this is required to reach us — it's stored locally on your device, as explained in Section 4.

3. AI receipt extraction & your own API key

When you photograph or upload a document and AI-assisted extraction is turned on, the image is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, authenticated with the API key you provided. This call is made using your own Google account and your own free-tier quota — Horizon Forge does not operate, proxy, or have visibility into this request.

Because this uses your own key, the data is governed by Google's terms for that product, not by an agreement between you and us. Google's published terms for its free-tier API state that prompts and outputs — which would include the receipt photo and the extracted text — may be used to improve Google's models. If you'd rather your documents never leave your device, you can turn AI-assisted extraction off entirely in Settings and use manual entry instead, which works exactly the same way and is always available offline.

We recommend reviewing Google AI Studio's terms of service before connecting a key, since that relationship is between you and Google.

4. Where your data lives

Your ledger, chart of accounts, customer and vendor lists, and captured documents are stored in a local database on your device. There is no Horizon Forge server that holds a copy of your books. If you back up your data, that backup file is created and saved by you, to a location you choose — we don't receive it.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your books are removed from the device along with it, unless you've saved a backup elsewhere first.

5. Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers, and the app does not display ads or contain third-party ad or tracking SDKs. The only data flow outside the app is the AI extraction call described in Section 3, which you control and which is made under your own Google account.

6. Subscription & billing data

Subscriptions are handled entirely through Google Play Billing. Horizon Forge does not receive or store your payment card details — that relationship, and any billing data associated with it, sits between you and Google. We only receive confirmation of subscription status (active, trialing, or cancelled) as needed to unlock the app's features.

7. App permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • Camera — to photograph receipts, invoices, and other documents.
  • Storage / files — to let you upload an existing photo or PDF, and to save exports and backups you create.
  • Internet — used only for the AI extraction call (Section 3) and for Google Play Billing/subscription checks. The app functions without it for capture, ledgers, and manual entry.

8. Your choices & controls

  • Use the app entirely offline in manual-entry mode, with AI extraction switched off in Settings.
  • Remove your Gemini API key at any time to stop AI extraction calls immediately.
  • Export or back up your ledger data locally whenever you choose.
  • Use "Clear all data" in Settings to permanently erase your business data from the device.

9. Data retention & deletion

Because your data is stored on your device rather than on our servers, it's retained for as long as you keep the app installed and choose to keep it, and it's deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. Any data sent to Google as part of AI extraction is retained according to Google's own policies for that product, not ours.

10. Children's privacy

Snap Bookkeeping & Accounting is a business finance tool intended for adults running or supporting a small business. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
