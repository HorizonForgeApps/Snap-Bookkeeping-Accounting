3. Accuracy of AI-extracted data

When AI-assisted extraction is enabled, the App uses a third-party AI model to read details off a photographed or uploaded document. The App is designed so that nothing posts to your ledger without your review and confirmation, and you're responsible for checking that extracted details are correct before confirming an entry.
